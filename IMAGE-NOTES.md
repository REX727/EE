# Teaching visuals

28 conceptual SVG diagrams are authored directly from the lesson concepts. Each has a bilingual explanation of what to observe. These are functional teaching drawings, not decorative generated circuits.

Two illustrative component photographs were made with the built-in image_gen tool, then saved into this website:
- assets/components.png — general component identification.
- assets/actuators.png — brushed gearmotor, RC servo and stepper comparison.

They are AI-generated identification illustrations, not evidence of an exact product, its scale, pinout or wiring. Website captions state this limitation.
